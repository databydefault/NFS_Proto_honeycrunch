    AI engine is configurable. This prototype includes a local document mode and can be connected to your organisation's AI endpoint.

    How it works

    1. 1
      Upload

      Add PDF, Word or Excel files. Text, tables and scanned pages are read automatically.

    2. 2
      Review the note

      A one-page summary with key figures and action items is ready to download as PDF or Word.

    3. 3
      Present, listen and ask

      Build a NITI-format deck from the source text, chart the data, listen in English or Hindi, and ask questions.

    What you get

    Summary note

    Context, key points, key figures and action items on one page. PDF and Word.

    Presentation

    Editable PowerPoint in NITI deck format, built from the original documents.

    Data insights

    KPI tiles and charts from workbooks and from figures inside reports.

    Audio and Q&A

    Listen to the full document in English or Hindi and ask questions about it.

    Summary note

    Crisp, one page where possible. Two pages only for large or multiple documents.

    Presentation

    Cover, contents, key findings, charts where the data allows, recommendations, next steps and annexure, in the NITI deck format.

    Data insights

    Every numeric column in an uploaded workbook becomes a KPI tile and a chart. These charts also go into the presentation.

    Audio reading

    The full document is read aloud in your browser. Hindi is translated section by section as it plays.
